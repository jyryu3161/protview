## Practical import contract

The initial protocol is `prolif_prepared_explicit_h@1`. User selects/uploads a prepared receptor PDB/mmCIF with explicit hydrogen coordinates, supplies complete covalent connectivity, uploads one ligand SDF pose, chooses the exact record and a ligand-instance identity, and acknowledges: supplied protonation/tautomer state is used; receptor bond orders/charges will be inferred; no protonation or optimization is performed. The default is not “assume pH7.” Store user-entered preparation description/pH as an unverified local annotation, not computed truth or outbound context. The UI describes missing requirements before submission.

Two supported ways to form the source:

1. Prepared receptor + SDF creates a new immutable `chemical_structure@1` assembled in the supplied common coordinate frame, with full links to both raw imports and their selected models/record. Retain receptor author/label/insertion identities exactly. New ligand identity is explicitly user chosen and collision checked; label fields not supplied are null. Generate stable unique ligand atom names from the SDF atom indices only when names are absent, and label that naming policy. Ligand H coordinates come from SDF, never AddHs. This is the practical path for the public tutorial fixture; it needs no preexisting combined structure or heavy-atom matching guess.
2. Attach SDF chemistry to an existing prepared complex and selected exact ligand instance. Require explicit bijection from SDF atom index/map tag to full M10 AtomRef for every existing ligand heavy atom; matching element, unique site and Euclidean coordinate discrepancy <=0.002 Å. Exact unique supplied atom names may propose a mapping, but ambiguous names/symmetry reject; show the full mapping before submit. No automatic conformer alignment. Existing ligand H must map and agree as well, or user explicitly selects replace-ligand-H-from-SDF, creating a new derived chemical source. Any new H has a new atom identity linked to the SDF; never reference an atom absent from source.

Receptor connectivity comes from complete PDB CONECT records or a separate `receptor_bonds@1` JSON upload containing a source handle and distinct pairs of authoritative AtomRefs. mmCIF without explicitly imported/validated connectivity requires that sidecar. User must declare connectivity complete. Symmetrize duplicate reciprocal CONECT records without multiplying bonds, reject self-bonds/out-of-source references/duplicate ambiguous serials, and preserve a full import audit. Do not infer connectivity by distance. Internally bonds initially have unspecified/single order for the versioned inference step, not claimed experimentally supplied bond orders. Residues/atoms may be selected only by exact source references. All receptor atoms are included; no implicit pocket crop or residue truncation.

Initial supported chemistry: standard protein residues plus explicit histidine aliases HSD/HSE/HSP and HID/HIE/HIP, elements H,C,N,O,S in receptor; ligand H,C,N,O,S,P,F,Cl,Br,I with sanitizable supplied covalent graph. No metals, covalent protein–ligand bonds, nucleotide/cofactor receptor, unresolved alternate locations, disconnected ligand, radicals, isotope-specific treatment, known missing backbone/required connectivity or detected partial-H inconsistencies. Retain/disclose unsupported inputs rather than silently excluding chemically relevant sites. Water is explicitly excluded and counted at import; other receptor nonprotein residues cause rejection. User-declared incomplete/uncertain hydrogens or detected H inconsistencies are preconditions, not a valid empty result. Strict native graph and explicit-H consistency checks below are required; merely finding at least one H is insufficient. They do not prove absence of all missing atoms. A chemistry mismatch must report offending exact residue IDs.

Validation is **chemical consistency of a supplied prepared graph**, not proof that its protonation or atom inventory is biochemically complete. Use pinned native mechanics: RDKit sanitization/strict valence checks after the declared MDAnalysis inference; require zero radicals, zero implicit hydrogens, zero atom-level bracket/explicit-H counts not represented as bonded H vertices, valid finite conformers, unique mapped atoms, and supplied bonds whose endpoints are present. Every H must have exactly one chemically allowed covalent neighbor; every reported donor H must be that actual bonded vertex. Reject isolated receptor heavy atoms, dangling missing endpoints, inconsistent supplied connectivity, missing protein backbone N/CA/C/O (except an explicitly documented terminal form), and detected valence/H inconsistencies. Verify representative amide/carboxylate/histidine chemistry with independent graph assertions. Do not claim these checks can detect every omitted H/heavy atom: an omitted atom may permit a different sanitized inferred charge/bond-order state. The user explicitly declares the supplied graph/H state complete for their intended prepared model; this declaration and the detection limitation are always displayed and reported. Inferred charge/bond orders remain inferred, even after validation.

For diagnostics, deterministic supplied receptor H-neighbor and zero-length covalent-vector faults are checked against staged exact AtomRefs before native inference. After native conversion succeeds and its parent-index bijection is verified, localized radical/implicit-H/isolated-site and inferred protein amide/cap/acid/backbone faults report a typed `receptor_graph_invalid` with exact offending source AtomRefs; localized selected-SDF graph faults use `ligand_graph_invalid` and SDF atom indices. Count/edge-set mismatch, parent-index failure, source hash mismatch and detect-graph reconstruction remain fatal provenance or invariant errors. MDAnalysis may fail during bond-order/charge inference or sanitization before returning its reordered RDKit molecule; such unlocalized failures remain fatal because an exception index cannot safely be mapped to a source AtomRef. Do not infer an identity from exception text or weaken pinned sanitization.

Standard protein residue names and listed histidine aliases are supported as supplied graph labels; names do not silently choose an idealized protonation state. Native and persisted graph checks require the five aromatic CG–ND1–CE1–NE2–CD2–CG ring edges, the single CB–CG attachment, exact ring heavy-neighbor sets, and a ring-H/charge pattern: one ring-N H with neutral ring, or two ring-N H with one positively charged N. HSD/HID, HSE/HIE and HSP/HIP must agree with that actual graph or reject a contradictory label; zero ring-N H is unsupported. Generic HIS records `neutral_ND1_H`, `neutral_NE2_H`, or `cationic_ND1_NE2_H` only when the supplied graph determines it. This is a represented graph state, not a pH or biochemical-completeness prediction. Native errors identify the exact source residue and implicated AtomRefs. Prepared terminal caps encoded within a named terminal residue are supported only as explicitly declared supplied covalent graph extensions, with cap atom inventory/connectivity shown in preview; no automatic cap removal, rebuilding or relabeling. Do not impose Amber atom-name/H-count templates or claim canonical ff14SB preparation: the official public fixture uses CHARMM-like naming (ILE CD rather than CD1) and a merged terminal cap (CAY/CY/OY). These are real supplied atoms, not missing/extra-atom errors to repair. Additional independent graph checks cover the supplied public terminal cap and these naming variants. Standalone nonprotein cap residues require an explicit future supported-input extension; unsupported metal/cofactor/covalent ligand restrictions remain.

For attach-existing mode, the exact selected ligand instance is explicitly excluded from receptor conversion and replaced/chemistry-mapped as declared; it is not rejected as an unexpected receptor residue. Additional ligand/cofactor instances require the user to prepare a source excluding them explicitly, or the request rejects. Retain the entire original complex as an immutable parent and enumerate every included/excluded source atom in the manifest. A receptor CONECT/sidecar bond to the selected ligand is covalent-complex unsupported, never silently discarded.

Do not implement OpenMM hydrogen generation, implicit-H ProLIF, generic protonation, complete arbitrary graph editing, or ligand parameterization in M11. Those were research alternatives, not requirements of this selected prepared-input lane. A strict externally supplied full graph can be added later; the initial method must openly describe its receptor order/charge inference.

## Actual conversion and method semantics

Build an MDAnalysis Universe from server-validated atom table, coordinates, supplied bonds and unique synthetic backend residues, avoiding a second ambiguous identity parse of receptor bytes. Set atom elements/names and all required topology fields explicitly. Convert with pinned `RDKitConverter`, `implicit_hydrogens=False`, `force=False`, `cache=False`, and `MDAnalysisInferrer(max_iter=200, sanitize=True)`. Capture inference warnings as bounded diagnostics; chemistry/valence failures reject. No guesser, arbitrary user callback, pickle or dill input. Explicitly assert all bond indices remain mapped through conversion. Source coordinates transferred into an MDAnalysis float32 representation can change at float32 precision; record actual engine conformer coordinates and discrepancy rather than claiming exact float64 equality. Each coordinate must remain within 1e-4 Å of the canonical source; reject coordinates outside supported magnitude instead of silently accepting larger drift. The prepared-public probe had 5053 bonds, all `is_guessed=False`; this independently supports the supplied-connectivity lane. [MDAnalysis converter](https://docs.mdanalysis.org/2.10.0/documentation_pages/converters/RDKit.html).

The selected first importer accepts V2000 SDF; V3000 is explicitly unavailable and must reject with a typed error. The capability, UI and report describe that limitation consistently. SDF is parsed with RDKit preserving H (`removeHs=False`), explicit bonds, charges and a single selected 3D conformer. Reject nonfinite coordinates, unsanitizable chemistry, missing explicit H required by atom valence, zero-length bonded vectors and unsupported stereochemical contradictions. Preserve supplied source coordinate precision and exact backend coordinates. No ligand bond-order inference from structure proximity.

The selected stereochemistry check is bounded, not a claim of complete stereochemical validation. For explicitly declared V2000 tetrahedral atom parity on supported degree-four centers, compare the pinned native assignment from that parity with the independently assigned configuration from the same indexed 3D coordinates; reject a contradiction or an unsupported/ambiguous declaration. Preserve a per-center preparation audit with the exact SDF atom index, declared parity, parity-derived CIP, independently 3D-derived CIP, signed 3×3 tetrahedral determinant in Å³ (six times signed tetrahedron volume; sorted SDF neighbor indices, first three coordinate vectors relative to the fourth), method version and match outcome; revalidate center coverage and coordinates before publication and on saved-object read. Follow RDKit's documented 3D treatment of ordinary wedge/dash depiction cues: the supplied 3D geometry controls their interpretation, so a changed wedge direction alone is not evidence of a changed 3D configuration. Disclose that rule instead of claiming all wedge directions were independently validated. Unsupported ambiguous/query stereo, atropisomeric declarations and other forms outside this explicit protocol reject. Alkene configuration inferred from coordinates is not relabeled as a separately supplied E/Z declaration. Actual tests must exercise the public declared centers, a parity-versus-coordinate contradiction and the documented wedge treatment. [RDKit 3D interpretation](https://www.rdkit.org/docs/RDKit_Book.html#interpretation-of-the-2d-3d-flag-and-stereochemistry).

Call the actual `prolif.Molecule.from_rdkit` + `Fingerprint(..., count=True).generate(lig, prot, metadata=True)` path. Fixed categories: HBDonor/HBAcceptor D···A cutoff3.5 Å, D–H···A130–180 degrees; Hydrophobic cutoff4.5 Å; Cationic/Anionic cutoff4.5 Å. Use exact 2.2.2 default SMARTS, stored with hash and full criteria payload; assert these thresholds against installed source. Vicinity cutoff6.0 Å, explicit H only, all qualifying occurrences. Native boundary comparisons remain those of pinned engine; tests probe both sides without redefining inclusivity. Native parent_indices are authoritative backend indices mapped through a checked bijection, not display residue-string parsing. [Interaction implementation](https://github.com/chemosim-lab/ProLIF/blob/v2.2.2/prolif/interactions/interactions.py), [metadata API](https://prolif.readthedocs.io/en/stable/source/modules/interaction-fingerprint.html).

`chemical_graph@1` persists every atom's full derived AtomRef, supplied versus inferred element/charge/aromaticity/H state, canonical bonds/order, actual engine coordinates, inference version/settings, conversion mapping and warnings. `chemical_preparation_manifest@1` records originals, selected source/model/SDF record, raw-byte hashes, complete mapping, excluded water counts, H policy, user preparation acknowledgement, precision and limitations. `chemical_structure@1` owns actual renderable coordinates. M11 adds a strictly validated chemical-source AtomRef resolver using the M10 reference shape for its own importer, assignments and local picking; that does not extend M10 tool source allowlists. No alteration of historical objects or accepted source-kind interpretation.

Object creation must be an acyclic content-addressed graph. Persist raw source/SDF/connectivity/choices first; then a preparation manifest referring only to those parent inputs and source atom references; then `chemical_structure@1` referring to that manifest; then the mapped chemical graph referring to the newly created chemical structure; then interaction result and report. The manifest's derived mapping uses stable local output site IDs, not an as-yet nonexistent chemical-structure handle. The graph expands those IDs into complete new AtomRefs. No object hash depends on its own handle or a later object's handle. Build all payloads in bounded worker output and publish successful objects coherently under existing durable runtime conventions; an interrupted job cannot report a completed chemistry calculation.

Use separate cache keys for preparation/conversion (raw inputs, model, connectivity, mapping, acknowledged states and method/dependencies) and detection (existing graph/source handles and method/dependencies). Do not require an output graph hash in the input cache key of its own conversion. User preparation comments stay immutable local metadata but cannot alter inferred scientific values except through explicit typed preparation decisions.

`chemical_interaction_set@1` links graph/manifest/structure and method/dependency manifest. Each deterministic row has stable local row ID; category/backend category; role-labeled atom references (D,H,A for hydrogen assignments); native parent/within-residue indices; `distance_A` with endpoint roles; optional `DHA_angle_deg`; and exact native metadata. Preserve all assignments, not first-match bitvectors. Charge proximity is labeled ProLIF Cationic/Anionic assignment; no energy. Empty valid native metadata yields `null_result=true`, zero rows and full provenance. Malformed indices, unexpected category, nonfinite or independently inconsistent geometry fail fatal parser validation, not scientific_null. Recompute every reported distance/angle independently from stored engine coordinates, with abs tolerance1e-6 Å/degree, verifying all role atoms. This validates mapping/geometry, not the truth of the protonation model.

## Runtime, UI and reporting

Register import/validation and calculation as distinct typed runtime operations. Raw uploads are bounded immutable local objects; conversion and fingerprint calculation use durable expensive jobs, normal explicit scientific approval, budget reservation before spawn, and charge failed attempts. Default maximum receptor10000 atoms, ligand256 atoms, total bonds20000, input10 MiB per file, output30 MiB, assignments20000, wall120s, process-tree RSS2 GiB. Reject above limits; do not silently truncate. Limit stderr/stdout to1 MiB each; terminate process group on exceed/timeout/cancel with 2s grace then kill. Single worker thread where libraries permit, no pools. Validate request before spawn. Parent revalidates output schemas/mapping/hashes before persisting results.

M11 adds a new one-heavy-worker limit per configured run root. Earlier phases supplied per-run ownership, not this cross-run limit; the previous prospective wording “existing global job concurrency cap” did not describe implemented historical behavior. The new limit must cover fixed OpenMM and chemical workers, acquire before budget reservation, keep status/cancellation responsive, and prevent an orphan worker from releasing the slot merely because its parent exits. Inherited lock ownership plus bounded durable reconciliation must fail closed when cleanup remains unresolved. A busy attempt is resumable and uncharged. This shared queue/guardian extension requires fresh Astra review and actual cross-process death, contention and cancellation evidence before M11 acceptance.

Only declared local files in a private job root are accessible via the protocol; environment is allowlisted without credentials/proxy settings. Production worker contains no HTTP, package manager or download path; actual integration tests deny external networking and verify no attempts. Any optional external preparation is outside runtime scope. Cancel, stale completion and crash cannot publish a partially valid result. Restart reconciles interrupted jobs with existing terminal semantics. Cache key includes immutable input/graph hashes, protocol and dependency manifest, normalized settings; same completed call never recomputes. Replay restores all raw/derived bytes, graph, results and reports from events with engine absent.

UI controls: prepared receptor upload/source/model; ligand SDF upload/record; create-new/attach-existing source; explicit instance/mapping preview; H replacement policy where relevant; preparation acknowledgement; capability/prerequisite status; submit/approval/job/cancel; result category filters and pagination; exact atom-row focus; show/hide assignment geometry; source return; immutable report download/history/reopen. Only enable Compute with valid prerequisites. Render D–H–A endpoints and distance connector from actual stored result/source; category selects exact ligand instance and receptor residue. Do not silently show assignment on an older source or comparison overlay. Use accepted M9/M10 native scene/generation guards; disable one-source save/export of transient annotation layers unless their schema explicitly supports them, with visible explanation. No model prompts/provider requests are introduced.

The M11 local chemical-source display is a new reviewed source-reader/binding lane, not an existing M8 viewer-intent session or M9 saved scene. It must validate the chemical structure kind/schema, same-run object ownership, manifest/source hashes, exact model and atom catalog before issuing a fresh local scene generation. Reuse native operation serialization and stale-source guards, but do not widen M8 `create_session` or its online projection. Chemical sources never become `prepared_structure` merely to pass an old allowlist. The M11 staging and chemical-structure atom catalogs may each return 1–1000 validated rows per page (default 100), against their existing 10,000-receptor-atom and 10,256-derived-source-atom caps respectively. M10/general catalog and chemical result-row page limits remain unchanged.

M9 `viewer_scene@1` retains its strict original source-kind set. While any `chemical_structure@1` is displayed, **disable Save view and PNG/sidecar export**, including when interaction annotations are hidden, with the reason “Chemical sources are not supported by saved-scene/export schema v1.” Existing saved scenes remain selectable and restore their supported historical source normally; that exits chemical display. M10 distance/comparison source selectors and APIs continue to reject chemical sources until a separately versioned/reviewed extension. Thus disable Measure and Add-to-comparison for chemical display, explicitly explaining the source-kind restriction. M8 prompt controls also remain disabled for that lane. Local chemistry row selection/camera/representation and chemistry report downloads remain available. A graph handle is never itself a renderable structure.

M11 acceptance must exercise these restrictions with annotations both on and off, direct API attempts using the new source kind, and return-to-supported-source restoration of M8/M9/M10 controls. Saved scenes, comparisons and figures of original/prepared/minimized sources keep their accepted behavior and bytes. Supporting chemical-source figures or comparisons in a later phase requires an explicit schema/reader/projection review and separate acceptance; it is not an implicit requirement or claimed result of M11.

Create `chemical_interaction_report@1` through existing field-specific evidence validation, citing exact result fields/units, criteria and inferred chemistry. Distances are D···A for H bonds; angle degrees; counts dimensionless. Native geometric contacts stay visible separately and may disagree. Preserve old report bytes and source snapshots. Reopen selects the saved chemical source and rows without converting/inferencing/running again.
