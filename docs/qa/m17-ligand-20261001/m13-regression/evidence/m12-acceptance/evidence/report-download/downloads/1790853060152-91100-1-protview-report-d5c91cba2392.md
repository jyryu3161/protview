## Limitations

- fpocket pocket candidates, ranks and scores are geometric detector output, not binding\-site, affinity or druggability evidence\.
- Score and Druggability Score are fpocket regression scores min\-max normalised across the pockets of the same execution; they are relative within one run and not comparable across runs or structures\.
- Volume is fpocket&\#x27;s Monte Carlo estimate with a clock\-derived seed \(seed control unsupported\); fresh executions can differ, and it is not an analytical union\-of\-spheres volume\.
- Total SASA is fpocket&\#x27;s point\-sampled surface of pocket\-contacting atoms not covered by the pocket&\#x27;s alpha spheres; it is not whole\-protein SASA or the result of the project&\#x27;s SASA tool\.
- display\_center is the arithmetic mean of stored sphere centers computed by ProtView, not an fpocket value\.
- Coordinates refer to the projected protein\-only transport input \(standard\-residue heavy atoms\)\.
- fpocket pocket candidates, ranks and scores are not binding\-site, affinity or druggability evidence \(결합 부위·친화도·druggability 증거 아님\)\.
- Druggability Score is an fpocket\-specific score, not druggability evidence \(fpocket 고유 점수이며 약물성 증거가 아님\)\.
- Display center: arithmetic mean of the stored alpha\-sphere centers, computed by ProtView for display only; it is not an fpocket value\.
